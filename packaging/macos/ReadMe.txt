Facet
=====

Facet records your time from a TimeFlip2 device. Turn the device, and the face that
lands up is what you are working on.

Installing
----------

Drag Facet onto the Applications shortcut beside it, then open it from Applications.

The first launch is refused, and that is expected. This build is not notarized by
Apple, so macOS says it cannot check it for malicious software. Open System Settings,
choose Privacy & Security, scroll to the bottom, and press Open Anyway beside Facet.
That is needed once. Every launch after it is normal.

Using it
--------

Facet lives in the menu bar. It has no Dock icon and no window until you ask for one:
right click the icon and choose Settings.

The first time it talks to your device, macOS asks whether Facet may use Bluetooth.
Say yes, or Facet cannot follow the device. You can change that later in System
Settings, under Privacy & Security, Bluetooth.

With no device paired, Facet times by hand: pick a category on the Faces tab and the
clock starts on it.

Your recorded time is kept in a database in
~/Library/Application Support/Facet
and nothing is sent anywhere unless you connect a Google account yourself.
